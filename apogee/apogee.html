Story

Juniper Dev Game Jam

Credit to Juniper Dev.

Around the end of Junior year, I proposed the idea of participating in a game jam to Seaver. We would have 7 days to make a game according to a specific theme. This theme would be announced the day of the event.

I also asked my friend Chessel Gibson to help, but he didn't really do that much.

Skyhook

Image from Stanford University.


The theme of the game jam was "Spin to Win", so naturally I had to make a game with lots of spinning.

Being a space afficionado, I've always been interested in SKYHOOKS. A skyhook is a kind of satellite that takes a load, spins around, and uses the rotational kinetic energy to rocket the payload further into space!


Development

Mid-development screenshot.


So, over the next 7 days, I made what would eventually be Apogee. Although I am crediting seaver and chess, I did most of the work. Seaver's music and sound arrived during the last 2 days, and chess made 2 sprites-- the earth and the basic skyhook. I did EVERYTHING else!!

It was so much stuff. The final day I worked the longest period of straight focus EVER. 22 whole hours-- from 5 AM to 3AM the next day.



Apogee Gameplay.


Results


Rating

Publicly, the project wasn't very good. Other players didn't understand the goal of the game, and some parts were too buggy. But, for a game made in only 7 days, it is very polished and I am very proud of it!

I truly think Apogee is the best game I've designed so far; its got a really solid foundation. All that's left is to clear up how to play and design a good tutorial. That will happen eventually, but not anytime soon.

There's so so much I can add to this game, and that feels so exciting!

Friend's Message


I sent this to a couple friends for playtesting, and most people understood it. One friend really enjoyed the game, which honestly made it all worth it. I don't care that other people didn't like it. My friend liked it a lot and even when only one other person enjoys your work, that alone makes it all worth it.

I don't regret a thing; this experience was great!
