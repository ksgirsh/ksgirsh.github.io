This is a website for... bingo?



To play, simply generate random moments until you fill out all squares on the bingo board! There is also a timer, to automatically randomize as desired.

You can edit squares in the "Bingo" tab. But also its fine if you use some other online bingo service too, the focus is more-so on the random moments. :)
